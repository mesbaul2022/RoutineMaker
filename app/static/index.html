🧭 How to Use Department Routine Maker — Fresh User Guide

Follow these 5 straightforward steps to configure your department and generate a verified routine from scratch or update an existing schedule.

Step 1 ⏰

Customize Department Time Slots

Review or customize your department's daily schedule: start time, class duration (default 50 min), classes per day, breaks (tea/lunch), lab block length, and working days. Default timings are pre-loaded so you can skip straight ahead if they already match.

Step 2 📦

Add Teachers, Courses & Rooms

Add faculty, courses, and rooms manually one-by-one from their tabs—or save time with the Bulk Import / Export Hub. Download pre-formatted Excel (.xlsx) or CSV (.csv) templates, fill them in Excel/Sheets, and upload everything at once.

Step 3 📋

Activate Terms & Assign Teachers

Open Assignments to toggle which batches (1-1 through 4-2) are currently running (ON vs OFF). Assign primary and co-teachers (Teacher 1 & Teacher 2) for each course. Optional electives automatically activate when teachers are assigned.

Step 4 🎯

Set Availability & Fixed Slots Optional

Need slot flexibility? Click Slots next to any teacher to block unavailable days/periods or mark them On Leave. On the Courses tab, pin fixed time slots for non-departmental courses (Chemistry, Physics, Math, EEE, ECE).

Step 5 ⚡

Generate, Verify & Export Routine

Head to Generate to run the CP-SAT solver. Choose Fresh Generation for a complete schedule or Freeze Mode to add a new term without disturbing running routines. Inspect Teachers, Labs & Rooms, and Batch grids in Master Routine.

🛡️ Built-In Scheduling Intelligence & Guarantees

Every generated timetable is mathematically validated before solving and independently audited afterward against institutional academic rules.

✅

Zero Clashes Guaranteed

Hard constraints strictly prevent teacher double-booking, room overlaps, and student cohort conflicts across all Sections (A/B) and Lab Groups (G1/G2).

⚖️

Teacher Workload Protection

Enforces daily period ceilings, caps teachers at at most 2 lab sessions/day (preferring 1/day), and prohibits consecutive back-to-back lectures of different courses.

🧪

Smart Room & Lab Allocation

Automatically matches Theory Homerooms, Software Labs, Hardware Labs (107/108), Seminar Room (501), Non-Dept Labs, and roomless Project/Thesis courses (CSE 3200/4000).

❄️

Incremental Freeze Mode

Already have published routines for running terms? Lock those batches in Freeze Mode so newly activated terms are scheduled around them with zero disruption.

📊

Multi-Perspective Master Views

Inspect the generated routine from three synchronized angles—Teachers Master Routine, Labs & Rooms Master Routine, and Batches & Sections—plus printable HTML & CSV exports.

📥

Flexible Bulk Excel / CSV I/O

Export live teachers, courses, and rooms anytime, or edit a single 3-sheet Master Excel Workbook and upload to synchronize your whole department instantly.

💡 Quick Tips for First-Time Users
  • Pre-loaded Sample Data Ready: The application comes pre-populated with a realistic department catalog (teachers, courses, rooms, and default time grid) so you can click Master Routine or Generate right away to explore how it works.
  • Use Excel/CSV for Fast Setup: If you are onboarding a fresh department or new semester, open 📦 Bulk I/O in the header, download the Master Excel Template, fill in the Rooms, Teachers, and Courses sheets, and upload it in one step.
  • Diagnosing Impossible Rules: If your manual slot restrictions or teacher workloads are mathematically impossible to satisfy simultaneously, the pre-solve validator and ablation engine will pinpoint the exact conflicting teacher, course, or rule.
